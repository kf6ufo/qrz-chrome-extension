QRZ Callsign Extension

Choose Look Up Their Callsign, Search My Logbook, or Sign Their Web Log from Chrome’s QRZ right-click menu.

For lookup or Logbook search, right-click a QRZ biography link (including callsigns on QSO pages) or a callsign link on the PSKReporter map. A recognized station link takes precedence over highlighted text. On other pages or links, highlight one complete callsign first.

To sign a Web Log, right-click a callsign in QRZ Notifications or a biography link beginning with https://www.qrz.com/db/. You can also highlight a callsign in plain text and right-click it.

Open extension Options to save an optional Web Log comment, such as Greetings from Colorado! 73s. Use one line of up to 171 characters. The example is a placeholder; comments are empty by default. Clear the field and click Save to sign without a comment again.

You can also reach Options by right-clicking the extension’s toolbar icon, or through its Details page at chrome://extensions. Your comment is saved locally in this Chrome profile and sent only when you choose Sign Their Web Log. Existing drafts are left alone; a comment exceeding a station form’s shorter limit stops signing instead of being shortened.

Logbook searches and Web Log signatures use your existing QRZ sign-in in this Chrome profile. After signing in, return to the selected callsign and try the menu again.
